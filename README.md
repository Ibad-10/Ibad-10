## About me

I'm a Computer Systems Engineering student at Brunel University London, ranked 1st of 30 with First Class in both years so far. I like work where software meets something physical: PLCs moving pallets, microcontrollers driving displays, or a camera moving through a rainy street.

- **Now:** Logistics Planning Intern (Controls & Automation) at **BMW Group**, programming PLC ladder logic in Siemens TIA Portal and WAGO e!COCKPIT for automated material flow.
- **Final-year project:** an AI model that sequences delivery routes at BMW Hams Hall to cut loading time and raise pallets per trip.
- **Before:** Data Analyst at **EY** (SAP Analytics Cloud) and Web Developer at **Cloud Nebula Enterprises** (Python, Dash).
- **On the side:** shipping [Pegboard](https://getpegboard.co.uk) and building animated scenes in Blender with Python.
